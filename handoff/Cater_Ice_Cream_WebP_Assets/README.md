# Cater Ice Cream WebP image pack

57 standalone high-resolution photographic recreations, with responsive WebP exports and 125 mapped placements across 19 page-template entries. Original approved logo and isolated mascot are included separately.

Start with CLAUDE_IMAGE_INSTRUCTIONS.md, then ASSET_MANIFEST.json and PAGE_IMAGE_MAP.csv. Open IMAGE_CATALOGUE.html to browse every photo and its page usage.

The approved designs and original palette remain unchanged. Photographs are recreated from flattened mockups rather than recovered original source files; they closely follow the direction but are not pixel-identical. The smallest screenshot thumbnails cannot become sharp full-size images through extraction or enlargement. Production use still requires factual product/brand review.

The pack does not include editable SVG icons, functional maps or page screenshots as website backgrounds. Those remain coded components in the handoff; their locations are recorded in the manifest. No new founder/staff identity photo is supplied.
